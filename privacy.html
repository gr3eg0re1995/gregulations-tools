Privacy policy

Last updated 4 November 2026

Who uses this app

Gregulations Tools is a private, single-user app. It is used only by the owner of the Gregulations YouTube channel to look at that channel's own data. It is not offered to the public.

What data it accesses

With the channel owner's permission, the app uses the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics API with read-only access to:

  • the channel's list of uploaded videos and their public details (title, length, publish date)
  • the channel's analytics reports, such as views, watch time, average view duration, subscribers gained and audience retention

It does not access any other Google account data, and it cannot upload, change or delete anything.

How the data is used and stored

The data is used only to review the channel's own video performance. It is stored on the channel owner's personal computer. The sign-in token is also stored there, and nowhere else.

The data is not sold, shared with or transferred to anyone, and is not used for advertising.

Google API Services

This app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. By using the app you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app's local files removes all stored data.

Contact

Contact the channel owner through the Gregulations YouTube channel.
